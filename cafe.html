Wigz Cafe

Wigz Cafe was started, as previously mentioned, from my former apartment on Wigglesworth street. After hosting a very successful cafe-themed 21st birthday party, I realized that this was something I wanted to do all the time, not just on my birthday. The following September, my roommate Viti and I hosted the very first Wigz Cafe. Wigz Cafe is a pop-up cafe concept hosted at my apartment. It is an event where guests are welcomed into my home to enjoy pastries baked by me, and handcrafted drinks whisked up by Viti. We ask that people pay just enough to cover our expenses and occasionally make a small profit that goes right back into the cafe! This weekend (November 2026) we will be hosting the 15th Wigz Cafe – we have run at least one every month since starting, and I am so excited! I really enjoy the opportunity to bake for others, but also the chance to spark community and quality time with friends. It has been a huge learning experience for me, and has become so meaningful that I have chosen to host a similar event, a community-centered Bake Sale, for my capstone project. The event information can be found here, and if you’re reading this, you’re more than welcome to attend!
