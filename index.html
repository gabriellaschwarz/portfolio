Hi, I'm Gabi.

I’m a 5th year Northeastern student studying Computer Science and Design. I am a passionate baker, and also currently work part time at Oakleaf Cakes Bakeshop. Although I’m originally from the Bay Area, California, I have grown to call Boston my home over the past few years. When I’m not studying or baking, I enjoy running, walking dogs, and doing arts and crafts, whether it's film photography, scrapbooking, or pottery. I started my Northeastern experience in the NU.in program, where I spent my first semester in Dublin, Ireland. I had an absolute blast exploring Europe with my new friends (who are still my best friends today!). Upon coming back to Boston, I was immediately planning my next semester abroad: my Junior year Fall semester in Rome, Italy. I ate about as much pasta as I possibly could, and of course stopped by my local gelato shop every night. My favorite flavor was Basil, Walnut, and Honey – something that took me quite some time to muster up the nerve to try, but once I did, I never looked back! Upon returning to Boston, I moved to Mission Hill to live in a house on Wigglesworth Street with my 5 best friends. The following Fall we started what would become one of the most valuable aspects of my college experience: Wigz Cafe. Click the menu above to learn more about Wigz!
